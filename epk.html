About

Pappelbaum is Nathan Vanderpool and Ronja Polzin. Between them: two guitars, two voices, and a shared instinct for songs that hold more than they say.

Their debut EP Each Eternal Day moves through entanglement, distance, and the slow process of letting go — five tracks that range from English to German, from the cellular to the cosmic. Vanderpool brings a decade of work at the intersection of sacred music and community; Polzin — also known for her ensemble project Maneko — writes with the precision of someone who chooses every word because it's the only one. The title track, written together, is where both sensibilities meet.

They are based in Berlin. They are not very good at promoting themselves. The music is very good.

The Artists

Nathan Vanderpool

Guitar, Voice, Songwriter, Arranger

Berlin-based musician and songwriter with roots in sacred music and a decade of work at the intersection of song, community, and contemplative practice. Alongside Pappelbaum, Nathan leads Agapic Chants and the Secret Chord Collective. Responsible for the arrangements on Each Eternal Day, he brings a patient, spacious touch to the architecture of the songs.

Ronja Polzin

Guitar, Voice, Songwriter, Filmmaker

Songwriter, vocalist, guitarist, and filmmaker also known for her ensemble project Maneko. Polzin wrote two of the five tracks on Each Eternal Day and co-wrote the title track with Vanderpool — and created all five of the EP's music videos. Her work moves fluidly between sound and image, and the two are inseparable in what Pappelbaum makes.
