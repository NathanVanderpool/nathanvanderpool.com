Secret Songbook

How it works

  1. 1

    Pick a song.

    Each page has the words, the chords, and a strip that shows how the song is built.

  2. 2

    Say if you're singing or playing.

    The page remembers your choice, your instrument, your capo, and your part for each song.

  3. 3

    Learn it, then sing along.

    Singers practice their line against Nathan's. Players get chord cards, sheets in their own key, and a chart to print.
